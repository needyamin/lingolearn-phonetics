Hear it. See the IPA. Practice out loud.

LingoLearn Phonetics is a free Windows desktop app for English pronunciation. Look up a word to hear it, read the phonetic transcription and a Bangla meaning, or open English Practice and read short paragraphs while the app listens on your PC.

Download for Windows View source

What it does

Built for learners who want pronunciation, phonetics, and Bangla together in one small app.

Speak

Play any word or phrase with Windows text-to-speech. Choose voice, rate, and volume in Settings.

IPA

See phonetic transcription from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary so you know how a word is said.

Bangla meanings

English-to-Bangla lookup from the bundled E2B dictionary, kept up to date from GitHub.

English Practice

Read a lesson out loud. The app marks matched words green and missed words red, then lets you continue.

Clipboard

Optional: copy text anywhere and the app can look it up or speak it. Turn this off in Settings.

Tray and shortcut

Close hides to the tray. Press Ctrl+Shift+L to bring the main window back, even from the tray.

History

Recent lookups stay on your PC so you can jump back to a word you already practiced.

On-device speech

Practice uses Whisper on your computer. No Google speech API. First use may download a local model.

English Practice

Open it with the book button on the main window. Speech recognition runs locally.

  1. Wait until the speech engine is ready (first run may download a Whisper model).
  2. Tap Hear to listen to the paragraph, if you want a model reading.
  3. Tap Speak, read one sentence, then pause for about half a second.
  4. Your transcript appears under the paragraph. Green matched; red was missed. Keep reading forward — do not repeat missed words.

A quiet room and a microphone close to your mouth work best. If the local model cannot load, the app falls back to Windows speech recognition.

Windows installer

Use the Inno Setup file LingoLearn-Phonetics-Setup-2.0.0.exe from GitHub Releases. Installs per user, Start Menu and Desktop shortcuts, no admin required unless you pick all-users.

Microsoft Store

The MSIX package is built for Partner Center. Store copies update through the Store. Dictionaries and lessons still refresh from GitHub.

github.com/needyamin/lingoLearn-phonetics/releases

How updates work

Materials

IPA, Bangla dictionary, and practice lessons check a few seconds after launch, then every few hours. Unchanged files are skipped.

The app itself

Store builds update in the Store. GitHub NSIS builds can download a new installer. Inno and portable copies need a new Setup exe; materials still update.

Privacy

No account. Lookups, history, and speech stay on your PC. We do not sell data.

Clipboard monitoring is optional. The microphone is used only while you tap Speak. Whisper runs on-device. The app may contact GitHub for dictionaries and updates, and Hugging Face once to download the speech model. Full details: Privacy Policy.
