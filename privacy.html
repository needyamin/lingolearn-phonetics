Privacy Policy

LingoLearn Phonetics · Effective 9 September 2026
Publisher: Md. Yamin Hossain

This policy describes how LingoLearn Phonetics (“the app”) handles information. The app is a desktop program for English pronunciation, IPA, Bangla meanings, and speaking practice. It does not require an account.

Information we do not collect

We do not operate our own servers for this app. We do not collect names, email addresses, payment details, advertising IDs, or analytics profiles. We do not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

  • Settings (voice, rate, volume, clipboard option) stay in local app storage on your PC.
  • Lookup history stays on your PC.
  • Dictionary and lesson files may be cached in the app data folder after an update.

Uninstalling the app removes the program files. Windows may keep an app-data folder until you delete it.

Clipboard (optional)

If you enable clipboard monitoring in Settings, the app reads text you copy so it can look up or speak that text. Copied text is processed only on your device. You can turn this off at any time.

Microphone and speech

English Practice uses the microphone only while you tap Speak. Speech is processed on your PC with a local Whisper model. We do not upload your recordings to a LingoLearn server.

The first time you use speaking practice, the app may download a speech model from Hugging Face (Xenova/whisper-base.en or a smaller fallback). That download is the model file, not your voice.

If the local model cannot load, the app may use Windows Speech Recognition. In that case, Microsoft’s privacy policy applies to how Windows handles speech.

Network use

The app may contact:

  • GitHub — to refresh dictionaries and practice lessons, and (for some installers) to check for app updates.
  • Hugging Face — to download the on-device speech model.
  • Microsoft Store — Store-installed copies are updated by Microsoft, not by GitHub.

Those services receive standard connection data (such as IP address) under their own policies. We do not receive a copy of that data.

Children

The app is a language-learning tool. It is not directed at collecting personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
